About

I am the co-founder and CTO of TurinTech AI, where I lead the engineering team behind Artemis, a code-optimisation platform that generates candidate changes, benchmarks every one on real hardware and ships only measured winners as pull requests — including optimisations merged into NVIDIA's TensorRT-LLM, OpenAI's Whisper and llama.cpp. I hold a PhD from University College London on Darwinian code optimisation: evolving programs under measurement to select data structures and code, published at ESEC/FSE, ICSE, ASE and SSBSE. My research since then is on evolutionary and LLM-guided search for software optimisation, and on making that search trustworthy enough for production code. Away from work I play 9-a-side football.
